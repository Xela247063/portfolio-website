About

I'm a second-year BBA Honours student at KUSOM, majoring in Finance. Most of what I've enjoyed so far happened outside lectures: interviewing managers at an AI company, working through HR problems for a Kathmandu BPO, and spending sixteen days in a village in Baglung running classes at two schools.

My interests run across financial institutions, business consultancy, investment and entrepreneurship. I haven't narrowed it down yet, and I'm fine with that for now. Longer term, I plan to study abroad.

Work

Project Prajna

Social Experience Project, Baglung

A sixteen-day community engagement at two partner schools in Batakachaur, Baglung, done with three teammates. We taught financial literacy and insurance literacy to grades 8 to 12, ran a personal branding class, and organised handwriting, quiz, art and volleyball competitions. We also gave out books, first aid kits and sports items, ran a sanitation and hygiene programme, and painted the outside of a school building.

Before leaving, I worked on sponsor outreach: storyboards for a short video, a presentation and a call pitch. NLG Insurance came on as sponsor.

Evolve Vue Pvt. Ltd.

Group HR project

Evolve Vue is a Kathmandu BPO serving U.S. healthcare clients. Our group started with the turnover problem and followed it through job descriptions, the recruitment pipeline, performance management (KPIs, rating scale, MBO, feedback) and a training needs analysis.

Fusemachines Inc.

Field research report

An IT-focused report on an AI company with Nepali roots. The primary data came from interviews with the IT Head, the PR Manager and the HR Manager, backed by secondary research on Nepal's AI and BPO sector.

Shop Wholly

Organisational behaviour report

A look at motivation and communication inside a small Daraz-based dry fruits business. We built a 25-question interview guide tied to Maslow, Herzberg, McClelland, Adams, Vroom, Bandura, Transactional Analysis and Hall's high and low-context framework, then wrote up the findings in APA style.

COIL project

International Business

A collaborative online project with a student at West Liberty University in the United States, as part of the International Business course.
